1. Binomial-to-Poisson Limit (10 points)

For a binomial random variable,

P[X = k] = C(n,k)pk(1-p)n-k, k = 0,1,...,n.

Consider n to infinity and p to 0 with lambda = np constant. Express the limiting PMF in terms of lambda. Identify the PMF.

2. Parity Transformation of a Poisson RV (10 points)

Let X be Poisson with parameter lambda, and define Y = 1 if X is even and Y = -1 if X is odd. Find the PMF of Y.

3. Best Estimate of a Bernoulli RV (10 points)

Let X be Bernoulli with parameter p, where 0 ≤ p ≤ 1. Let the constant estimate be X-hat.

(a) Find the best estimate. (b) Find its mean-square error in terms of p. (c) For what p is the error maximal, and why?

4. Binomial Characteristic Function (15 points)

For a binomial random variable X: (a) calculate its characteristic function; (b) determine its expected value and variance from that characteristic function; and (c) show it becomes the characteristic function of a Poisson RV in the limit n to infinity, p to 0, with lambda = np constant.

5. Q-Function Identity (5 points)

For a PSK digital communication system, the probability of error has the form Pe = Q[square-root of 2E/N0], where N is standard normal. Prove Q(x) = P[N > x].

6. Affine Transformation of a Gaussian RV (5 points)

Let X be N(0,1) and Y = 2X + mu. Prove that Y is N(mu,4).

7. Squared Standard Normal RV (5 points)

Let X be N(0,1) and Y = X2. Find fY(y).

8. Clipped Rayleigh RV (10 points)

Let X be Rayleigh and define Y = min(X,xmax): Y = X for 0 ≤ X < xmax, and Y = xmax for X ≥ xmax. Find the distribution of Y.

9. Exponential Characteristic Function and Moment (10 points)

Let X be exponential with rate lambda. (a) Calculate its characteristic function PhiX(omega). (b) Calculate the n-th moment E[Xn] using the characteristic function.

10. Rare Events and Importance Sampling (20 points)

Let X be N(3,1). (a) Express P[X > 8] as an integral, and estimate the number of realizations needed to observe 10 such events. (b) For fY(y) = exp[-(y-3)]u(y-3), estimate the number of realizations needed for 10 events Y > 8. (c) Using importance sampling, express P[X > 8] as EY[h(Y)] and find explicit h.
